## 3. Edge Cases and Boundary Handling

- **Deployment Missing `TOKEN_KEY`:** Both `/auth/microsoft/start` and `/login/sso/<orgId>` fail fast with 404 without emitting an unsealed/fallback state or querying the database.
- **Hub with Discrepant Key:** When [`CalendarHub`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-booking/service/src/calendars.ts#L108-L150) uses a key distinct from `config.tokenKey`, `deps.calendars.state` takes precedence, preventing state open failures on round-trip return.
- **Unconfigured / Missing SSO Organization:** Unregistered orgs and orgs lacking `org_sso` configurations reject with `'This organization has no SSO configured.'` without leaking deployment metadata.
- **External IdP Failures:** Network/discovery errors on `discoverOidc` retain their 502 status response.
- **Isolation of Calendar Flows:** `/auth/google/start`, `/app/calendar/<provider>/connect`, and calendar OAuth callback routes preserve their existing gates and fallthrough behaviors verbatim.
