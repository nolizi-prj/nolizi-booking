## 2. Specification & Implementation Verification

### A. Public Sign-up, Identity & Oracle Hardening (SPEC-0002 §4.1: I2, I7, I8, I9, I10)
- **Email Proof Required (I7):** Public sign-up creates the account without issuing an immediate session cookie; a single-use sign-in link is emailed, preventing account takeover via unverified email inputs.
- **Oracle Elimination (I8, I10):** `POST /signup` on the public path returns identical 200 responses whether the email is newly registered or already taken.
- **Rate Limiting & Routing Enforcement (I9):** Rate limits (5 sign-ups/IP/hour) are enforced at the directory/router layer (`service/src/worker.ts` & `service/src/directory.ts`).
- **Verification Token Consumption (I10):** The `/v/` verification endpoint marks the intent token as consumed before executing the booking, preventing token replay attacks.

### B. Subprocessor Disclosure & Ceilings (SPEC-0002 §4.6: D1, D6)
- **Enforcement Without Outage (D6):** Configuring an unlisted SMTP host causes `start()` to log a loud warning and wrap mail transport in `RefusingMail` (with `RetryingMail` queuing delivery), keeping booking surfaces operational while ensuring no personal data leaves to undisclosed third parties.
- **Raisable Defaults (D1):** Ceilings (5 owner accounts, 200 bookings) function as safe defaults while allowing operators to explicitly configure higher capacities.

### C. Custom Questions & Personal Data Boundaries (SPEC-0002 §4.6: D2, D9)
- **Label Snapshotting:** `booking_answers` records a snapshot of the question label at submission time, ensuring subsequent edits to question text do not alter the historical record of what was asked.
- **Bounded Inputs & Cascading Deletion:** Answers are capped at 2,000 characters; answers are deleted on both booker deletion (`/b/:token/delete`) and owner account purge (`/app/delete`). Removing an active question preserves existing answered records.
- **Privacy Disclosure:** `service/src/legal.ts` correctly discloses organiser-controlled custom questions, and `service/test/legal.test.ts` enforces that all form inputs match legal notice disclosures.

### D. Branding & Analytics (SPEC-0002 §4.6: D2, D3, D5)
- **Logo Validation:** `service/src/branding.ts` enforces strict magic-byte signatures for PNG, JPEG, and WebP (rejecting SVGs to prevent stored XSS) and caps decoded size at 32 KB.
- **Timezone-Aware Analytics:** Analytics calculations are computed in Temporal in the owner's timezone rather than SQL dialect-specific timezone operations, maintaining SQLite and PostgreSQL parity without exposing PII.

### E. Safety, Dialect Parity & Documentation
- **UI Safety:** Account deletion has been moved from routine event views to the Settings page and styled with distinct danger tokens.
- **PostgreSQL / SQLite Dual Migrations:** All schema additions (`018_custom_questions.sql`, `019_branding.sql`) have matching SQLite migrations (`014_custom_questions.sql`, `015_branding.sql`).
- **Documentation Parity:** `SUBPROCESSORS.md` and `README.md` accurately qualify the difference between Node SMTP and Workers Gmail API transports, and uncited claims in `core/ENGINE.md` have been corrected.
