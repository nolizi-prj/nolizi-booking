## 1. Intent Statement Fidelity & Scope Discipline

[`SPEC.md`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-booking/service/spec/0007/SPEC.md) accurately specifies each requirement, constraint, and default assumption established in [`INTENT.md`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-booking/service/spec/0007/INTENT.md) ([CHARTER §2.1](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi/governance/CHARTER.md#L134-L158), [`L-005`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi/lessons/L-005-review-checks-coherence-not-intent.md)):

- **Microsoft Sign-in Reachability (`S2a`–`S2d`):** Gating `/auth/microsoft/start` on `!states || !config.msClientId` rather than `deps.calendars` allows self-hosted deployments with Microsoft OAuth credentials (and no Google Calendar) to complete end-to-end sign-ins without expanding OAuth scopes beyond `openid`, `email`, and `profile`.
- **Org SSO Reachability (`S3a`–`S3d`):** Gating `/login/sso/<orgId>` on `!states` removes the Google Calendar dependency for enterprise identity federation across both Node and Workers Durable Object execution paths.
- **Guard Order & Refusal Copy Preservation (`S2b`, `S3b`, `S3c`):** The deployment seal check is strictly executed prior to querying the `org_sso` table, preventing information leakage of registered org IDs on unconfigured deployments. Refusal strings (`'Microsoft sign-in is not configured.'`, `'SSO is not configured on this deployment.'`, `'This organization has no SSO configured.'`) remain unchanged.
- **Explicit Scope Boundaries:** No new OAuth providers, scopes, or endpoints are added. Known non-blocking discrepancies (omission of `TOKEN_KEY` in refusal copy, `worker.ts:596` Google secret check) are correctly isolated in §5.

## 3. Edge Cases & Boundary Handling

- **Deployment Missing `TOKEN_KEY` (`NO_KEY`):** Both `/auth/microsoft/start` and `/login/sso/<orgId>` fail fast with 404 without emitting unsealed state tokens or inspecting database records.
- **Hub with Discrepant Key (`HUB_OTHER_KEY`):** `deps.calendars.state` takes precedence over `config.tokenKey`, ensuring states open successfully upon callback.
- **Missing or Unconfigured Org SSO:** Non-existent org IDs and orgs without configured `org_sso` rows cleanly reject with `'This organization has no SSO configured.'`.
- **Calendar Flow Isolation:** Non-regression of `/auth/google/start`, `/app/calendar/<provider>/connect` (which returns 200 by falling through to the dashboard on hub-less setups), and calendar OAuth callback 404s are preserved verbatim.
