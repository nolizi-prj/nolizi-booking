## Cal.com is the better product for most people

**Cal.com** is more complete than this catalog item. It is a mature,
production-proven, feature-complete scheduling platform (`DUPLICATION.md` §3).
Its core is **AGPL-3.0**, with a commercial `/ee` tree beside it (open core).

**For anyone who does not require a permissive license, Cal.com is the better
choice.** Use it.

Pumasi states this plainly so it does not compete by omission
(`DUPLICATION.md` §5.3).

## Why this item exists anyway

Charter commitment **C1** (`CHARTER.md` §1.2 / `charter.yaml` `core.C1_license`)
fixes the catalog at **Apache-2.0**, inbound-equals-outbound. C1 is unamendable.

AGPL-3.0 cannot be relicensed to Apache-2.0, cannot be vendored into an
Apache-2.0 work, and cannot be forked into the commons (`DUPLICATION.md` §3).
Cal.com is structurally unavailable to Pumasi, not technically deficient.

The gap filled here is therefore narrow: **a permissively-licensed scheduling
core**, for embedding, commercial redistribution, and agent-assembled
composition (`DUPLICATION.md` §3, §6). That is a license constraint, not a claim
of superiority over Cal.com.

## Re-evaluation

At `Working` maturity, re-evaluate. If a permissively-licensed core appears in
the interim, this item is a deprecation candidate (`DUPLICATION.md` §5.4).
